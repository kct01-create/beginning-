Built from real materials, not trends

We started Clasp because most accessories are an afterthought — thin plating, glued seams, clasps that give out in a month. We design the other way around: pick the material first, then the shape. Everything is finished by hand in small batches.

A piece from Clasp is meant to be worn on rotation for years, not just for a season's photos.
